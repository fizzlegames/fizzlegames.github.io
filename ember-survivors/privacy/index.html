Last updated: October 2026

Fizzle Games ("we", "us") operates the mobile game Ember Survivors (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our App.

Information we collect

The App itself does not require account creation or collect personally identifiable information directly. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob – used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store In-App Purchase / Unity In-App Purchasing – used to process in-app purchases. Purchase transactions are handled by Google Play or Apple; we do not store your payment information.

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging – used to send push notifications. It may collect a device push token and basic device information to deliver notifications.

Tracking (iOS)

On iOS, the App asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalized advertising. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads may still be shown but will not be personalized using the advertising identifier. You can change this choice at any time in your device Settings.

Push notifications

The App may ask for permission to send push notifications, for example about game updates. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Local data storage

The App stores game progress, settings, and currency balances locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

Third-party privacy policies

For more information about how our third-party partners handle data, please review: Google · Google AdMob · Firebase · Apple · Unity

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

About the game

Step into a world consumed by fire and chaos. Ember Survivors is a fast-paced top-down survival action game: fight off endless hordes of monsters, level up on the fly and build an unstoppable hero with every run.

  • Endless waves – monsters grow stronger the longer you survive
  • Level up & choose your power – attack, armor, speed, range and more
  • Guardian enemies – rare, powerful guardians protect hidden treasure
  • Characters & maps – unlock new heroes and battlefields, each with its own challenge
  • Gold & diamonds – earn them by playing or watching optional reward videos

How to play

  • Just move – steer your hero around the map and your hero attacks on its own.
  • Collect XP from defeated monsters, level up and pick a new ability each time. Every choice shapes your build.
  • Take down guardian enemies for extra rewards, but don't let the horde surround you.
  • Spend gold and diamonds on new heroes, maps and upgrades, then try to survive even longer.

Oyun hakkında

Ateş ve kaosla kaplı bir dünyaya adım at. Ember Survivors, yukarıdan bakışlı, hızlı bir hayatta kalma aksiyon oyunu: bitmeyen canavar sürülerini püskürt, oynarken seviye atla ve her turda durdurulamaz bir kahraman kur.

  • Sonsuz dalgalar – hayatta kaldıkça canavarlar güçlenir
  • Seviye atla ve gücünü seç – saldırı, zırh, hız, menzil ve daha fazlası
  • Muhafız düşmanlar – nadir ve güçlü muhafızlar gizli hazineleri korur
  • Karakterler ve haritalar – her biri kendine özgü yeni kahramanlar ve savaş alanları aç
  • Altın ve elmas – oynayarak veya isteğe bağlı ödüllü videolar izleyerek kazan

Nasıl oynanır

  • Sadece hareket et – kahramanını haritada yönlendir, saldırıyı o kendiliğinden yapar.
  • Yenilen canavarlardan XP topla, seviye atla ve her seferinde yeni bir yetenek seç. Her seçim karakterini şekillendirir.
  • Ekstra ödüller için muhafız düşmanları yen, ama sürünün etrafını sarmasına izin verme.
  • Altın ve elmaslarla yeni kahramanlar, haritalar ve güçlendirmeler aç, sonra daha da uzun süre hayatta kalmayı dene.
