Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy describes how "War Survivor Last Stand" ("the app"), published by Fizzle Games, handles information.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email or precise location. Game progress (gold, diamonds, unlocked characters and maps, best scores, settings) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Third-party services

The app uses third-party services that may collect and process certain data:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): may collect the advertising identifier, device information, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, including optional rewarded ads.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications): uses a device/installation identifier and push token to deliver push notifications.
  • Google Play Billing / Unity IAP (in-app purchases): purchases are processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS). We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Unity Gaming Services: may process device information required to operate in-app purchases.

These services have their own privacy policies: Google · Google AdMob · Firebase · Unity · Apple

Advertising

The app shows ads. On Android you can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). On iOS the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier is used; if you decline, ads may still be shown but will not be personalized. You can change this choice at any time in your device settings.

Push notifications

The app may ask for permission to send notifications, for example about game updates. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Data deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. You can remove locally stored data by uninstalling the app. For any request, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version will be posted on this page.

About the game

Darkness has fallen. Endless waves of skeletons, trolls, minotaurs and dark mages are closing in — and you are the last one standing. Slash through the hordes, collect XP gems, pick new powers as you level up and survive as long as you can.

  • 4 unique heroes – Warrior, Fantasy Knight, Horseman and Orc Assassin
  • Combos & skills – 3-hit combos, arrow storms, explosive damage, wind auras
  • Boss fights – a stronger boss arrives every few minutes
  • 7 maps – from the Grassland to the Crimson Keep and the Cursed Swamp
  • Permanent upgrades – Health, Damage, Armor, Magnet and Gold Gain
  • Daily missions, achievements and free gold & diamond chests

How to play

  • Move your hero with the on-screen joystick (on Mac, use the keyboard – keys are shown on the buttons).
  • Tap the attack button to swing; keep tapping to chain a 3-hit combo. Use the skill buttons for special attacks.
  • Defeated enemies drop XP gems – collect them to level up and choose a new power each time.
  • Stay alive when the boss arrives, collect gold and diamonds and spend them on new heroes, maps and permanent upgrades.

Oyun hakkında

Karanlık çöktü. İskeletler, troller, minotorlar ve kara büyücüler dalga dalga üzerine geliyor ve ayakta kalan son savaşçı sensin. Sürüleri biç, XP elmaslarını topla, seviye atladıkça yeni güçler seç ve olabildiğince uzun süre hayatta kal.

  • 4 farklı kahraman – Savaşçı, Şövalye, Atlı Süvari ve Ork Suikastçı
  • Kombolar ve yetenekler – 3'lü kombolar, ok yağmuru, patlayan hasar, rüzgâr aurası
  • Patron savaşları – birkaç dakikada bir daha güçlü bir patron gelir
  • 7 harita – Çimenlik'ten Kızıl Kale'ye ve Lanetli Bataklık'a
  • Kalıcı güçler – Can, Hasar, Zırh, Mıknatıs ve Altın Kazancı
  • Günlük görevler, başarımlar, ücretsiz altın ve elmas sandıkları

Nasıl oynanır

  • Kahramanını ekrandaki joystick ile hareket ettir (Mac'te klavyeyle oynanır, tuşlar butonların üstünde yazar).
  • Saldırı butonuna dokunarak vur; art arda dokunursan 3'lü kombo yaparsın. Özel saldırılar için yetenek butonlarını kullan.
  • Ölen düşmanlar XP elması bırakır; topla, seviye atla ve her seviyede yeni bir güç seç.
  • Patron geldiğinde hayatta kal, altın ve elmas topla; bunlarla yeni kahramanlar, haritalar ve kalıcı güçler aç.
